## 2. Identity

- **Name (wordmark):** `grihostiOS` — lowercase `grihosti`, uppercase `OS`
- **Tagline:** `Home • Land • Production`
- **Primary Domain:** `grihosti.xyz`
- **Positioning line:** *"A Modern Operating System for Home, Land & Production"*
- **Brand promise:** *"Distinctive. Modern. Memorable."*
- **Bangla line:** "আপনার জমি, আপনার নিয়ন্ত্রণ" — "Your Land, Your Control"

### Wordmark treatment
- `grihosti` in **white** (on dark) / **plum** (on light)
- `OS` in **Fresh Green** — always the accent, never recolored
- Tagline sits beneath in a lighter weight, letter-spaced, separated by `•`

## 4. Palette — Option 5 "Plum & Green"

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Plum | `#3B1E3F` | Primary / Brand — default dark canvas |
| Mauve | `#8B5CF6` | Secondary accent, gradients, UI highlights |
| Fresh Green | `#22C55E` | Accent — primary CTA, `OS` in wordmark, success |
| Mint | `#E9F7EF` | Surface / Background — light surfaces, emblem field |
| Slate | `#374151` | Text / Neutral — body text on light surfaces |

**Rules**
- Deep Plum is the default canvas for hero and marketing surfaces.
- Fresh Green is reserved for the primary action and the `OS` accent — do not dilute it.
- Mauve is a supporting accent only; never use it for primary CTAs.
- Rationale: *Plum gives a premium and unique identity. Green adds a fresh, productive feel. Together they create a balanced, contemporary and trustworthy look.*

## 6. Composition Language

The hero key art establishes a repeatable pattern:

1. **Left column** — emblem + wordmark, headline with green emphasis words, single green pill CTA (`PRIMARY ACTION ›`)
2. **Right column** — product-in-context: dashboard UI, mobile app, and property photography layered in perspective
3. **Background** — deep plum with large, low-opacity botanical/leaf line art bleeding off-canvas
4. **Floating chips** — small rounded glass cards with icons (home, analytics, land) scattered for depth

### Product UI shown
- Sidebar nav: Dashboard, Properties, Land, Production, Finance
- Overview stat cards: `28 Properties (+12%)`, `12 Production Units (+8%)`
- Recent Activity feed (e.g. "STFL House — Rent collected")
- Mobile app: map view with property pins + stat summary

## 7. Design Illustration System

Flat-vector, warm, community-forward illustration style. Consistent traits:

- **Palette-locked** — plum, mauve, green, mint only; no off-brand hues
- **People-centric** — diverse, active, collaborative figures
- **Botanical framing** — leaves and plants in foreground/background
- **Every scene carries the logo lockup** in the top-left corner
- **Soft daylight** — bright, optimistic, no harsh shadows

## 10. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Keep the plum canvas + green accent pairing
- Keep the emblem's four motifs intact
- Show real product UI in marketing compositions
- Maintain the `Home • Land • Production` tagline
- Use the illustration system for all scene-based visuals

**Don't**
- Recolor `OS` away from Fresh Green
- Place the wordmark on busy photography without a scrim
- Introduce new accent hues outside the five tokens
- Use the emblem without its circular field
- Mix illustration styles — the flat-vector system is the only approved style
